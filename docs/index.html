Travel Fieldnotes is an open-source Codex skill for researched, illustrated travel guides. Give it your dates, interests, budget and fixed plans. It helps turn them into something useful—and worth reading.

$travel-fieldnotes

Installation and requirements · Source on GitHub

These are fictional public examples, not personal travel plans or bookable packages. Budgets are labelled planning allowances; availability and prices need checking for your dates. Sources, image credits and practical assumptions are included in each guide. Cover photographs: A.Savin, Wikipedia (Ibiza, Free Art License); Vyacheslav Argenberg (Bali, CC BY 4.0).
