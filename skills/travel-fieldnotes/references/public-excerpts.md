# Public excerpts and promotional media

Apply only when the user requests sharing, a showcase or a public demo. Publication itself requires appropriate user authorisation.

## Rebuild from an allowlist

Create a new public directory. Select individual approved story passages, public POIs, licensed images and synthetic examples. Rebuild those excerpts with new neutral headings. Never copy the private guide directory and try to redact it later. Do not embed a full private PDF behind blurred pages or upload it to a video service.

Exclude names, family details, account names, origin/home clues, exact travel dates, booking references, flights, reservation links, work schedules, private events, future attendance, exact accommodation choices and stay pins. Use neutral sample cities or public landmarks when those are authorised. Remove query parameters containing dates, guests, search state or identifiers. Replace private QRs with verified public destinations or omit them.

## Rights and honesty

Verify public reuse rights for each image, map, typeface and audio track. Keep required attribution accessible in the artifact or its supplied credits as the licence requires. Use original text and motion; do not copy another creator's launch script. Say “sample pages” when pages are reconstructed. Do not advertise stale prices, untested capabilities, guaranteed outcomes or personal attendance.

## Video craft

Lead with the result, then show how a short brief becomes that result. Use large readable text and purposeful motion. Keep the principal message understandable without sound. Give detailed pages time to register; zoom into a meaningful detail instead of racing through unreadable thumbnails. A call to action should tell viewers what to do, not promise virality. Check current platform specifications from official sources before encoding.

## Final public audit

Inspect rendered frames across every scene and transition, plus first/last frames. Review all onscreen text, captions, source bundles, metadata, audio, URLs and decoded QRs. Search for personal strings from the private brief without saving those strings into the public package. Inspect image EXIF, PDF title/author fields and archive members. Check filenames, browser chrome and background tabs. Use a second reviewer when authorised; otherwise do a separate privacy pass. Keep private inputs and audit notes outside the public directory. Deliver a clearly identified safe-to-share subset; never imply the entire working folder is public-safe.
